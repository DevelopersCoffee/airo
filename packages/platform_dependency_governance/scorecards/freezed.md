## Licence

MIT.

## Why this dependency

Not a choice. `flutter_rust_bridge_codegen` **requires** it: a Rust enum with
data — `ProcessingEvent`, which carries the streaming pipeline's progress —
generates a Dart sealed class whose implementation is freezed's. Codegen fails
with `MissingDep` without it.

The alternative is not "write it by hand"; it is "do not stream typed events
across the bridge".

## Blockers

**None.** `development_only`, zero shipped bytes.

## Risk

Build-time only, so a defect is a broken build rather than a broken device. The
generated output is committed to `.gitignore` and regenerated by
`melos generate`, which means a supply-chain concern surfaces at generation
time and is reviewable in the diff of what it produced.
