The name is Coins. The product is not a cryptocurrency. Airo Coins does not offer a token sale, investments, returns, banking, money transfer, or financial advice.

The direction

Less financial noise. More personal control.

Important records are often spread across apps, screenshots, notes, and inboxes. Airo Coins is being shaped as one focused, private place to organize the essentials without turning them into a feed.

01

Locked by default

The planned journey starts at a clear vault boundary—not an open dashboard of sensitive details.

02

Masked by default

Summaries stay useful while sensitive values remain hidden until an intentional reveal.

03

Local-first by design

Local control is the baseline. Any future sharing or network behavior must be explicit and separately qualified.

Planned first experience

A vault that explains itself.

The first product slice is deliberately narrow: unlock, scan grouped summaries, add a record, reveal only what you need, then return to a locked state.

  1. 01
    Unlock deliberately

    Enter through a device-protected gate with clear unavailable and recovery states.

  2. 02
    Scan safe summaries

    See grouped records without loading full sensitive values into the list.

  3. 03
    Reveal only when needed

    Inspect or copy a selected value through a short-lived, explicit interaction.

  4. 04
    Lock on the way out

    Backgrounding, timeout, or manual lock closes the session boundary.

These outcomes describe the public Phase 0 direction. They remain in development until a published release proves availability.

Clear boundaries

Trust starts with what we do not claim.

A product handling financial records should be precise about its role. Airo Coins is an organization and vault direction—not a replacement for a regulated institution or professional advice.

Not an investment or tokenNo coin sale, tradable asset, yield, return, or value promise.
