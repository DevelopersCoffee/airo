Strategic Principles

Guardrails & Monetization Rules

Rules for paywall presentation, feature gating, and target economics.

Keep from Strategy Brief

  • Freemium First Funnel

    A hard paywall before playlist import kills the 26% store-to-install conversion funnel. Allow users to test import first.

  • Lifetime as Cash Engine

    BYOP users already pay ISP and playlist providers; they purchase media players as durable infrastructure.

  • Five-Device License Cap

    Unlimited device licenses turn into shared family/friend accounts. Cap at 5 devices per license.

  • Local-First Low Cost Structure

    Local-first processing means free tier users carry near-zero infrastructure cost. A 1–5% paid conversion delivers strong margin.

  • Single Account Unlock

    One Google Play account unlocks phone, tablet, and TV automatically (matching existing user install behaviors).

Reject or Tighten

  • Do NOT use $1.49 monthly

    That is a budget price slot. Cheap monthly pricing lowers brand perception.

  • Do NOT use $24.99 lifetime

    Too close to budget tier ceilings ($19.99–$25.99). You lose the premium peer signal for just $5.

  • Do NOT skip annual tier

    Without a $9.99/yr anchor, $29.99 lifetime lacks a clear decoy to demonstrate value.

  • Do NOT default to monthly on paywall

    Default the paywall to Lifetime ($29.99) to maximize upfront cash generation.

  • Do NOT sell "premium" as ads-off only

    Gate utility features: multi-view, multi-playlist, favorites sync, catch-up, and import intelligence.

Play Console Execution

Play Console & RevenueCat Resolution

Addressing Play Store SKU activation gaps to resolve RevenueCat Error 23 runtime billing failures.

Root Cause: Missing Priced Base Plans

Product aika_stream_premium exists in Google Play Console, but currently has no active priced base plans configured. This causes RevenueCat billing lookup to throw Error 23 (Invalid Product Identifier).

  1. Configure Subscription Base Plans: In Google Play Console under aika_stream_premium, add base plan monthly ($2.99/mo) and base plan yearly ($9.99/yr).
  2. Create One-Time In-App Product: Create standard in-app product aika_stream_premium_lifetime priced at $29.99.
  3. Link RevenueCat Packages: Map the Play products to RevenueCat default offering packages: $rc_monthly, $rc_annual, and $rc_lifetime.
